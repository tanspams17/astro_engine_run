Questions people actually ask

Is this a real calculation or a template?

Real. We compute your chart with the Swiss Ephemeris, the same astronomical data professional astrologers use, from your exact birth moment and coordinates. No two reports come out the same.

Will you charge me again?

No. You pay once and get one report. There's no subscription running in the background, and nothing to remember to cancel.

What if my birth time is approximate?

Use your best estimate. Sun, Moon and planet signs are usually unaffected; your rising sign may shift if the time is off by more than an hour or two.

Refunds?

If something's wrong on our end, we'll fix it or refund you. Just reply to your delivery email. Because each report is generated for you individually and delivered right away, we can't offer change-of-mind refunds after that. Full policy: terms & refunds.
