Before you ask

Is this a real calculation or a template?

Real. Your chart is computed with the Swiss Ephemeris from your exact birth moment and coordinates. No two reports are the same.

Will you charge me again?

No. One payment, one report. There is no subscription, no trial that converts, no account to cancel.

What if my birth time is approximate?

Use your best estimate. Sun, Moon and planet signs are usually unaffected; your rising sign may shift if the time is off by more than an hour or two.

Refunds?

If the report is wrong because of an error on our side, we'll fix it or refund you — reply to your delivery email. Full policy: terms & refunds.
