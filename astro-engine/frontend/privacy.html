Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Arvelos, astro.arvelos.cloud · support@arvelos.cloud

What we collect and why

Birth details (date, time, place): used solely to calculate your astrology report. We treat these as personal data and never share them with advertising or analytics providers.

Name and email: used to personalize and deliver your report, and to respond if you contact support.

Phone number (optional): only if you choose to provide it, used solely as a support reference — never for marketing, never shared.

Campaign parameters (UTM tags in the link you arrived from): used in aggregate to understand which of our content brought you here. These are not tied to your birth details in any analytics tool.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't run third-party analytics scripts on the quiz. We don't store your card details — payment is handled entirely by our payment provider (Stripe or Razorpay), on their own secure page; we never see or touch your card number. We don't email you marketing unless you explicitly ticked one of the optional boxes at checkout — general updates, and monthly Rashi/zodiac insights are separate opt-ins, both unticked by default — and every such email includes an unsubscribe link that works.

Retention

Order records are kept as required for accounting and dispute handling. We also keep a simple customer record (name, email, phone if given, and which of the optional emails you're subscribed to) so we can help you if you have an issue with a past order. You may request deletion of your birth details, report, and this customer record at any time by emailing support@arvelos.cloud — we will delete them within 30 days, keeping only the minimum needed for accounting (order date, amount, and status), with everything that could identify you removed.

Your rights

Under GDPR/UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Email support@arvelos.cloud to exercise any of these. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Hosting

Our service runs on servers in the EU. Reports and order data are stored there.
